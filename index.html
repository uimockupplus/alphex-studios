Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll reply quickly.

How long does a website take?+

A landing page usually takes 3–5 days. A business website typically takes 7–12 days. E-commerce and custom projects depend on scope.

How much does a website cost?+

Landing pages start at ₹6,999, business websites at ₹14,999, and e-commerce stores at ₹39,999. We share a fixed quote after understanding your requirements.

Do you use AI to build websites?+

Yes. We use AI to speed up research, wireframes, content drafts, and development. Our team reviews, refines, tests, and finalizes every website.

Do you have client projects yet?+

Alphex Studio is newly launched. We are currently accepting a limited number of founding-client projects. Our website, process, and concept designs demonstrate our approach; client projects will be added with permission after launch.

Can you redesign my existing website?+

Yes. We can improve the design, speed, mobile experience, SEO, and conversion flow without losing your existing content or search rankings.

Do you provide maintenance?+

Yes. Our care plans include updates, backups, security checks, uptime monitoring, and content edits.
